How it works

Stay on budget where spending actually happens.

1
Set a budget amount.
Choose the total you want to keep an eye on. Tellava tracks how much you have left as you log spending over time.
2
Save your monitored stores.
Add the shops, cafes, or chains that tend to steal your budget. Tellava watches for those locations in the background.
3
Update in the moment.
When Tellava sees you near a monitored store, it prompts you to enter what you spent so your budget stays current — not just in theory, but based on real visits.
Privacy & permissions, in plain language.

Tellava works best when you grant “Always Allow” background location so it can detect nearby monitored stores and send budget alerts before impulse spending happens. Budget amounts and spend prompts stay local to your device, users remain anonymous, and only transient location data is used for proximity matching outside the app.

Tellava does not sell or share personal information for advertising. For full details, please see the published Privacy Policy on tellava.ca when available.
